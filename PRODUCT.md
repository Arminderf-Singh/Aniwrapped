# AniWrapped

**Anime tracking, narrated as a story.** Spotify Wrapped's annual retrospective format applied to a user's MyAnimeList history: stats, charts, taste profiles, and activity heatmaps arranged as a personal chronicle rather than a utility dashboard.

## Register

brand

## Users

Anime fans (ages 16–30) who actively maintain a MyAnimeList account and watch 20–150+ titles per year. They share Wrapped results on Twitter and Discord, so every section must be screenshot-worthy. They know Spotify Wrapped's emotional rhythm: anticipation → discovery → nostalgia → pride.

## Product Purpose

An any-time reflection on a user's anime journey. The goal is not to expose every data point, but to create a sequence that feels like a small gift from your past self. Every screen earns its place by surfacing something the user didn't fully realise about themselves.

## Brand

Ink-and-paper Japanese aesthetics. Warm cream base (#fdf6ec), sakura pink as the emotional primary (dominant in 30–40% of colored moments), amber for energy highlights, dusk purple for depth, moss for grounding. The palette reads like the worn pages of an anime artbook: warm, loved, slightly amber-tinted.

Typography: Playfair Display for display moments (large, authoritative, nostalgic). Sora for body (clean, modern, airy). Noto Serif JP for Japanese accent text (authentic, contemplative).

Tone: editorial warmth. Not clinical data. Not hype. Quiet celebration of time you chose to spend watching stories.

## Anti-references

- Spotify Wrapped's gradient vomit (dark mode, neon, fast-paced dopamine loops)
- Anime tracker dashboards (MAL's own site: dense tables, zero personality)
- Generic SaaS analytics (dark blue + purple gradients, hero-metric templates)
- Glassmorphism panels used decoratively

## Strategic Principles

1. **Narrative over data**: order and language create a story arc, not just numbers
2. **Screenshot-worthy**: every section should stand alone as a shareable image
3. **Warm, not frantic**: slow reveals, gentle animations, cream grounds — this is quiet celebration, not a TikTok scroll

## Scene

A fan opens AniWrapped late at night, desk lamp casting warm amber over their screen. Half-expecting familiar numbers (episodes, hours) and half-surprised by what the taste profile reveals. They screenshot the archetype card. They share the heatmap. The experience must feel this personal and this quiet.
